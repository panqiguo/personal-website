
Foundational belief  基础信念

I believe games and game genres are poorly defined and full of immense untapped potential. We are artists living in a frontier space that is ripe with the opportunity to create great new work within. Games should not be treated as an established or rigid form. 
我相信游戏及其类型定义模糊不清，充满巨大的未开发潜力。我们是生活在一个充满机遇的边疆空间中的艺术家，有机会在此创作出伟大的新作品。游戏不应被视为一种既定或僵化的形式。

Recurring themes  反复出现的主题

世界是从故事推演出来的，在想象中也许他和某个机制融洽，融合。

故事是进入的理由，也是游戏的目的，机制是制造感情的机器。

* Grid and tile-based systems: I have a dot grid notebook. It helps me think through designs.  格网和瓷砖系统：我有一个点格笔记本。它帮助我思考设计。

* Clear / simple rules of cause and effect. I’m not really a big complex simulation person. 清晰/简单的因果规则。我不是一个喜欢复杂模拟的人。

* Loose level design: Our games often have messy procedurally generated levels whose goal is only to add in base variance. I’m not a good level designer and have little sense of space. Then I lean upon the mechanics to make the game interesting, no matter how crappy the level design.  松散的关卡设计：我们的游戏经常有杂乱的程序生成关卡，其目标只是增加基本的变异性。我不是一个擅长关卡设计的人，也没有空间感。然后我依靠机制来使游戏有趣，无论关卡设计多么糟糕。

Anti-influences  反向影响
Some influences, I run away from. Other people can love these topics! I just don’t.  有些影响，我避之不及。其他人可以喜欢这些话题！我却不这么认为。
* Disney: Crass Americana consumerism. The cartoon style, the wide-eyed heavily censored manipulative stories targeted at traditionalists and children, the hard nosed extractive machinery of the media and the theme parks.  The miserable empty suburbanism of it all. 迪士尼：粗俗的美国消费主义。卡通风格，充满洗脑色彩且严重删减的故事，针对传统主义者和儿童，媒体和主题公园的榨取机器。那种可悲的空洞郊区生活。
* Early Console games: Early console games involved game planners creating a ride for players. They focused on fail/retry loops, level design with golden paths and gating progress via puzzles or bosses. I’m allergic to almost all of these design patterns. They take autonomy away from players and produce blocked states where you throw down in frustration the thing you once loved. 主机游戏。早期主机游戏主要是由游戏策划为玩家创造一个游玩体验。他们专注于失败/重试循环、带有金色路径的关卡设计以及通过谜题或 Boss 限制玩家的进度。我对这些设计模式几乎过敏。它们剥夺了玩家的自主权，并且会产生让你沮丧的停滞状态，让你曾经喜爱的东西变得不再有趣。
* Puzzles: I hate puzzles with a passion. The single solution type. Authored by a designer. 谜题：我痛恨谜题。单一解法的谜题。由设计师创作。
* Action: I’ve never had great reflexes and I have no intuition or patience for try/fail/retry gameplay. These are better designed and played by someone else. 动作：我的反应速度一直不好，也没有尝试/失败/重试 gameplay 的直觉和耐心。这些游戏更适合别人来设计和游玩。
* Games for kids: I have zero interest in making games for children. And correspondingly lots of interest in making games for thoughtful adults who live complex, intensely human lives.  面向儿童的游戏：我对制作面向儿童的游戏毫无兴趣。相反，我对制作深思熟虑的大人玩的游戏非常感兴趣，这些大人过着复杂而充满人性的生活。
* One-off content: I’m semi-allergic to expensive bespoke stuff that is used once and never again. I am improving here! But still have a clear bias towards appropriate reuse and high leverage on content (while avoiding oatmeal)  一次性内容：我对昂贵的一次性定制内容有些过敏。虽然在这方面有所改进，但我仍然明显倾向于适当的重复使用和高效率的内容利用（避免使用无用的内容）

Process  过程

原型制作，我会使用AI结合来制作最初的原型，探索机制/风格/故事的融合，以及故事该如何发展

* Design-led strategy: At this point in my career, there’s usually a plan for the whole game. I practice visualizing the long term end state of the design, then add the starting steps and finally plot out the least risky ways to connect the start to the end. There’s an architecture to any design and an order of operations in how to optimally build the game. I only rarely design like a truffle pig, rooting for fun.  设计主导的战略：在我的职业生涯中，通常会有一个关于整个游戏的计划。我会先想象设计的长期最终状态，然后添加起始步骤，最后规划出最安全的方式将起点与终点连接起来。任何设计都有其架构，而构建游戏的最佳顺序也是一套操作流程。我很少像寻找松露的猪一样，仅仅为了乐趣而设计。

* Turns of the crank: Designs are structured around inevitable iteration. New systems or content invest a lot in that first iteration. Subsequent iterations focus on clarity, efficiency, standardization, variation and scaling to production.  迭代的过程：设计通常围绕不可避免的迭代进行。新的系统或内容会在第一次迭代中投入大量资源。后续的迭代则专注于清晰度、效率、标准化、变化和生产规模的扩展。

* Content leverage: We seek leverage on our content. Reuse, variations, proc gen, deep mechanics that are amenable to these things. So at first we optimize for fun, but rapidly start thinking about potential. 内容的杠杆效应：我们寻求在内容上获得杠杆效应。通过重用、变化、生成式内容以及易于实现这些效果的深层机制。因此，我们首先优化乐趣，但很快就开始考虑潜在的可能性。

* Creating knobs: As we create tools and prototypes, always thinking about knobs we can tune those systems with. So that as we get more gameplay feedback, it is easy to adapt to. I hate building things that need to be thrown away during balancing. 创建旋钮：在开发工具和原型时，总是考虑可以调整这些系统的旋钮，以便在获得更多的游戏反馈后能够轻松调整。我讨厌那些在平衡过程中需要被丢弃的东西。

* Team trust: All this can only happen with a high trust, high collaboration, high skill team. I’d rather work with a small group of high skill crafts folk than a big group of cogs who need micromanagement.  团队信任：这一切都只能在高度信任、高度协作、高技能的团队中实现。与其管理一大群需要精细管理的齿轮，我更愿意与一群高技能的手艺人合作。


Topics I’ve explored  我探索过的主题
These are interesting problems that have fascinated me over the years. I’ve explored more but these are major ones. The important part is the burning curiosity that drives the creation of clever ‘solves’ in space full of impossible constraints.  这些问题多年来一直吸引着我。我探索了许多方面，但这些都是主要的问题。重要的是那种强烈的求知欲，它驱使着在充满不可能约束的空间中创造出巧妙的解决方案。
* Non-violence: What if we made games without guns or combat? Just toss all those mechanics. What else could we make? 非暴力：如果我们制作没有枪械或战斗机制的游戏会怎样？干脆把这些机制都扔掉。我们还能制作什么类型的游戏？
* Content leverage: How do we get more bang-for-buck from our expensive content? See proc gen, content standards, tooling, trickle narrative.  内容利用：我们如何从昂贵的内容中获得更多的回报？见程序生成、内容标准、工具以及涓流叙事。
* Coziness and cuteness: What makes things cute? What is the adult version of positive emotions that enriches us instead of infantilizes us? 温馨与可爱：什么让事物显得可爱？什么是能够丰富我们而不使我们幼稚的成年版正面情绪？
* Pointillist narrative: How do we paint the world through many intense modular snippets vs linear narratives? 点彩叙事：我们如何通过许多强烈的模块化片段来描绘世界，而不是线性叙事？
* How do innovators survive or thrive? How do we keep making impactful, interesting new games in a hostile, increasingly conservative creative and economic environment? 创新者如何生存或繁荣？在日益 hostile 和保守的创意和经济环境中，我们如何继续制作具有影响力和趣味性的新游戏？


Upon reflection, there was an ongoing ‘cozy’ thread in our early games (Triple Town, Road Not Taken, Alphabear). I was also becoming increasingly intrigued by multiplayer (RotMG, Leap Day, Steambirds Alliance) Also, the pain of the More-ish period finally (to David’s palpable relief) convinced me I needed a new set of production and risk management tools. And we’d bounced around from genre to genre excessively and weren’t able to build team momentum. 
经过反思，我们早期的游戏（如《Triple Town》、《Road Not Taken》、《Alphabear》）中存在一种持续的“温馨”氛围。我也越来越对多人游戏产生了兴趣（如《RotMG》、《Leap Day》、《Steambirds Alliance》）。此外，《More-ish》时期带来的痛苦终于（让大卫明显松了一口气）让我意识到我需要一套新的生产和风险管理工具。我们还频繁地从一个游戏类型跳到另一个游戏类型，无法建立起团队的持续动力。
So my next set of designs tried to lean into all these factors.  所以我的下一组设计尝试充分利用了这些因素。
This period starts in a wobbly fashion with Beartopia and Alphabear Hustle. And continues with more confidence through to Cozy Grove and our next game. I think of them as bigger projects in the life sim space with less design risk at their foundation. But still some big bets. And a concrete goal of training the studio to become excellent at making a recognizable genre.  这一时期以《Beartopia》和《Alphabear Hustle》的摇摇晃晃开始，并通过《Cozy Grove》和我们下一款游戏，逐渐变得更加自信。我认为它们是生活模拟类游戏中的大项目，基础设计风险较小，但仍是一些大胆的尝试。并且它们有一个明确的目标，即训练工作室成为制作可识别类型游戏的高手。
